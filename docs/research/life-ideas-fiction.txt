METHOD AND LIMITS
- Original 200-entry books.json was read before selection. Normalized-title comparison found no duplicate original titles; new IDs are unique.
- Coordinated title ownership with the foundations and engineering agents to avoid overlap.
- Every source URL below was returned by web research from a publisher, author/estate, or university. No source URL was generated from a guessed book slug.
- Every source URL was also submitted to a direct web open. 95 returned book-page content with matching title information. Five returned access/timeout errors; those exceptions are identified individually below. Search-index previews still identify the corresponding books. These are not falsely labeled successful fetches.
- This is bibliographic and curatorial verification, not a claim to have read all 100 complete books or independently validated every scientific assertion within them.
- Publisher descriptions establish book identity, authorship, subject, and edition context. They are promotional sources, not independent proof of scientific or political claims.
- Rationale paragraphs, questions, and aerospace/robotics connections are original editorial interpretations. They are not quotations or claims of publisher endorsement.
- All 100 rationale paragraphs contain 35–60 words (observed range 46–57).
- Publication years normally identify the original work rather than the currently linked edition. Meditations is null because of ancient composition/publication ambiguity. Fiasco is null because the first translation and original-language publication dates differ. The Machine Stops and Other Stories is the 2024 collection; its caveat separately dates the title story to 1909. Beggars in Spain and Flowers for Algernon identify the expanded novels, not their earlier short versions.
- Copyrighted passages are not reproduced. Book notes are concise original prose.
- Fiction is marked fiction and framed as thought experiments, not engineering forecasts. No operational weapon construction or harmful biological instructions are provided.
- Longevity titles are comparative reading, not an immortality promise or personal medical regimen. Lifespan and Ending Aging explicitly identify speculative/contested claims and limits of human evidence.
- Thiel and Karp/Zamiska are included alongside Arendt, Scott, Ostrom, Mill, Popper, Jasanoff, Vallor, and Ellul. Their arguments are identified as arguments rather than neutral consensus.
- No private biographical, financial, family, location, or schooling details are included in public catalog entries.
