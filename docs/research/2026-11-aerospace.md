# Aerospace edition source register

Reviewed 2026-11-01. Exercises, mission connections, prerequisites, and reading order are editorial teaching material. They do not represent an accredited curriculum or current enrollment advice.

- [NASA Astrobee](https://www.nasa.gov/ames/astrobee/) supports the ISS robotics example. The mission page distinguishes station-interior research from free-space inspection.
- [NASA ARMADAS](https://www.nasa.gov/automated-reconfigurable-mission-adaptive-digital-assembly-systems-armadas/) supports ground-based modular assembly research. Orbital shipyards remain a conditional horizon in this atlas.
- [NASA aeronautics guide](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/) provides educational context for forces, flight, and propulsion.
- [NASA Earth Fact Sheet](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/earthfact.html) provides the equatorial reference radius, 6378.137 km, and rounded gravitational parameter, 398600 km³/s², used in the ideal two-body model. This is an illustrative circular-orbit model with drag, oblateness, third bodies, and maneuvers excluded.
- [NASA Systems Engineering Handbook](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/systems-engineering-handbook/) supports systems and verification context. The satellite mass and energy allocations are invented teaching numbers.
- [OpenStax Prealgebra](https://openstax.org/details/books/prealgebra-2e) and University Physics [Volume 1](https://openstax.org/details/books/university-physics-volume-1) / [Volume 2](https://openstax.org/details/books/university-physics-volume-2) are legal foundations resources. The atlas writes its own examples.
- [Feedback Systems author site](https://www.cds.caltech.edu/~murray/FBS/Second_Edition.html) supplies a control reference. Search indexing confirmed the author-hosted page; direct retrieval returned an intermittent 502 during review. The model is an independently stated first-order proportional loop with an exact analytic solution, no overshoot, and nonzero steady-state error for finite positive gain.
- [UCSD Computational Flow Physics teaching](https://flowphysics.ucsd.edu/teaching/) was directly retrieved. It lists Gerhart/Gerhart/Hochstein's 8th-edition fluids text for MAE 101A, Anderson's 6th-edition aerodynamics text for MAE 104, and Anderson's 1st-edition CFD text for MAE 185 and MAE 290C. This evidence is scoped to the retrieved page; the site does not claim every future section uses these editions.
- [Wiley thermodynamics listing](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/fundamentals-of-engineering-thermodynamics-9th-edition-p-978111939138804) confirms Moran, Shapiro, Boettner, and Bailey's 9th edition through indexed publisher content. Direct retrieval returned 403. No course-adoption claim is made.
- Pearson's [Hill/Peterson propulsion](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/mechanics-and-thermodynamics-of-propulsion/P200000003466) and [Versteeg/Malalasekera CFD](https://www.pearson.com/en-gb/subject-catalog/p/Versteeg-An-Introduction-to-Computational-Fluid-Dynamics-The-Finite-Volume-Method-2nd-Edition/P200000005670?view=educator) pages were retrieved for author and edition metadata.
- McGraw Hill's [Modern Compressible Flow](https://www.mheducation.com/highered/product/modern-compressible-flow-with-historical-perspective-anderson.html) page was retrieved for Anderson's 4th edition. The aerodynamics catalog retains its stable record and publisher source, whose current edition differs from the dated course listing.
- [JPL Electric Propulsion](https://descanso.jpl.nasa.gov/SciTechBook/st_series1_chapter.html) was directly retrieved and provides authorized chapter downloads for the original Goebel–Katz volume. It is not labeled as the newest edition.

The companion contains selected external references and links to existing catalog records. It does not change the catalog's 500 selections, inflate its count, or duplicate saved book IDs. Source links may change and no price or availability guarantee is made.
