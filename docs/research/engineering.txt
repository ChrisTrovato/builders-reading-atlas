RESEARCH AND VERIFICATION
All100 titles and author attributions were checked against first-party publisher, author, NASA, or university evidence using web search and, where needed, direct page inspection. Most records point to an individual publisher book page. Exceptions are explicitly labeled author sites, official textbook sites, publisher samples/front matter, NASA records, or a university textbook catalog. No retailer-only URLs were substituted for these sources.

The source checks establish bibliographic identity and support topic/scope descriptions. They do NOT mean that every book was read in full, that every factual claim inside a book was independently validated, or that every URL was exhaustively tested through all browser and regional variants. Some first-party evidence came from indexed publisher/catalog text rather than an unblocked full page. Publisher and author descriptions are promotional sources; the original relevance notes and caveats deliberately frame the books as material to think with, not authorities whose arguments must be accepted.

Original200 was read before selection and checked for exact duplicate titles. Separate editions were not counted as separate books. Overlap in subject is intentional where authors offer materially different approaches; users are explicitly told not to buy every overlapping spacecraft-control reference.

YEAR POLICY
Publication year is nullable. When first-publication year or edition identity was ambiguous, year was left null instead of guessing. Numeric years are supported by primary bibliographic/catalog evidence. Links can point to a later reprint while the displayed year identifies the original work. Textbook editions should be checked before purchase. In particular, Computer Architecture links to the fifth edition to keep the listed two-author attribution consistent; the seventh edition has an additional author.
